01 / OVERVIEW

Real-world motion.
Large-parallax perspectives.

RealDynLFV is a benchmark for real-world dynamic light field video with large parallax. It brings together dynamic scenes captured across 5 × 5 synchronized camera views, supporting research in light field video processing and interpolation.
