01 / OVERVIEW

Real-world motion.
Large-parallax perspectives.

RealDynLFV is a large-parallax benchmark for real-world dynamic light field video. It comprises 45 dynamic scene sequences recorded from a 5 × 5 camera array. Camera recordings are temporally aligned in post-processing using audio signals; the cameras are not hardware-synchronized.

CAPTURE & ALIGNMENT

Audio-based temporal alignment in post-processing. No hardware synchronization.

EVALUATED TASKS

Reconstruction & coding

The benchmark has been evaluated for light field video reconstruction and coding. Detailed evaluation settings and results will be presented with the paper and accompanying resources.

POTENTIAL APPLICATION

Video interpolation

Light field video interpolation is a potential application. It is distinct from the reconstruction and coding tasks evaluated in the current work.
